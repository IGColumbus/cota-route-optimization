# outputs/

The study's artifacts. Start with `CANONICAL_RESULTS_v5.json`, the registry of
which artifacts are current; v1–v4 are kept unchanged.

## Layout

* **Canonical artifacts stay at their `research-final` paths** (`cd03af9c`).
  The registry, the freeze manifests (`GEN1_FREEZE_MANIFEST.json`,
  `exp3/FREEZE_MANIFEST.json`) and the experiment contracts name them by
  path, so they were not gathered into one folder. `outputs/canonical/` is an
  older folder that holds three records written there at the time
  (`exp1_final.json`, the Exp 1 headline record, and the two pre-Exp 3 baseline
  records); it is not the full canonical set.
* **Superseded artifacts:** most moved on 2026-10-05 to `outputs/superseded/`
  (same sub-paths). `SUPERSEDED.md` indexes all of them, with a table of
  current locations at the top; four sets were not moved
  (`outputs/model_A/`, `outputs/exp4/run/certified/`, `outputs/exp3/superseded/`,
  `outputs/exp3/observations_cert.superseded/`).
* **Run logs:** moved on 2026-10-05 to `docs/research-record/run-logs/`
  (same sub-paths). They are forensic history, not results.
* **Everything else** under `outputs/` (checkpoints, heartbeats, intermediate
  JSONL) is the run record of the experiments, kept in place.
* **Report figures:** `docs/report/figures/`, generated by
  `scripts/make_report_figures.py`.

edited into it.

  is not listed, `superseded` is empty, and the headline quotes the superseded
  +0.0065% (Exp 2 errata E2).

  (`SUPERSEDED.md`, Experiment 3 sections).
* **exp1:** the Model A seed-check's `seedcheck.csv` and `seedcheck.jsonl` are
  not listed alongside `seedcheck.json`. The exp1 `headline` still ends with the
  withdrawn "197.0 of 197.0 peak vehicles"; the correction is in the same
  entry's `reporting_corrections` block (report §9 R6).
* Paths in the registry are as of `research-final`; `SUPERSEDED.md` and
  `docs/research-record/MOVES.md` give current locations.
