# Experiment 7 — robustness of findings F1–F6: CLOSEOUT

**Status:**

* Stage 1: **`EXP7_STAGE1_EVALUATION_COMPLETE`**.
* Stage 2: **`EXP7_STAGE2_REOPT_COMPLETE`**.

Completed 2026-10-04. Experiment 7 asks how far the findings of Experiments 1–6
move when the model's assumptions move. It answers in two stages, under the
final production contract (`docs/EXPERIMENT7_AMENDMENT.md` §14):

* **Stage 1** holds every certified plan fixed. It re-evaluates each plan at
  every implemented assumption level.
* **Stage 2** re-optimizes. It does so only in the two assumption dimensions
  that a preregistered metric selected from the Stage 1 results.

The machine-readable record is:

* `outputs/exp7/EXP7_CLOSEOUT_TABLE.{json,md}`, generated by
  `scripts/exp7_closeout.py`;
* `outputs/exp7/stage1/EXP7_STAGE1_ANALYSIS.json`;
* `outputs/exp7/EXP7_ANALYSIS.json`.

Tables below are transcribed from those artifacts. `docs/EXPERIMENT7_RESULTS.md`
is the shorter results note.

**The one-paragraph answer.** Three findings survive:

* **Frequency redistribution:** fixed plans keep their reduction in unserved
  demand under every perturbation tested.
* **The greenfield network:** it is worse than the redesign at every level
  where the objective is well posed.
* **Policy prices:** they keep their sign at almost every level.

Re-optimization exposes the objective's own boundary. With no operating-cost
term, the λ-weighted objective prefers dropping a rider to serving them in two
situations:

* when the unserved penalty (λ × 60 min) falls below the marginal served trip's
  generalized cost;
* when perturbations raise the generalized cost of the harder trips.

At λ = 1, re-optimization collapses service. Under higher transfer or walking
costs, it sheds riders. So F1 is robust **for the plans that were certified**,
and **not** as a property of re-optimizing under any calibration.

## 2. Design

**Stage 1 — fixed-plan evaluation.**

* **Registry:** 60 frozen solutions from Experiments 1–6 (59 evaluable; N3 R1_H20
  has no plan), re-evaluated unchanged on four network variants (N0, N3, N4, and
  N0S, the Exp 2B splice).
* **Levels:** BASE plus every implemented level, giving **192 evaluation cells**.
* **Emptiness proofs:** 13 N3 R1_H20 proofs at the A3 and A7 levels.
* **Bootstrap:** all 20 LODES bootstrap draws were evaluated.
* **Sharing:** no search and no plan sharing.
* **Authority:** Stage 1 is authoritative for F1–F6 and AF1.

**Stage 2 — adaptive re-optimization** (D39-safe):

* Initial solves at every included level.
* Then a basin closure on each track, with these components:
  * within-level (W) anchor transfers along the policy nesting graph;
  * dimension-local cross-level (X) transfers, with BASE as the only hub;
  * the same certifier;
  * a ceiling of 8 passes.
* **Tracks:**
  * **F6 track:** 14 policy cells on N0 and N3, feeding F6, AF1 and F1 adaptive.
  * **F4 track:** REF only, on N0, N3 and N4.
* **Sentinels:** four, re-run in reversed order.
* **Firewall:** a per-level contract binds every reported comparison.

**Selection metric** (preregistered before any Stage 1 result):

* score(d) = max(movement_F1(d), movement_F4(d)) over the Class A dimensions.
* The top two are selected, with ties broken by dimension id.

| dimension | score |
|---|---|
| A5 | 1.196 |
| A6 | 0.685 |
| A1 | 0.415 |
| A7 | 0.414 |
| A3 | 0.152 |
| A8 | 0.124 |
| A2 | 0.094 |

A2 was not selected, so the five-draw bootstrap re-optimization subset was never
invoked.

**Classification** (Sept 23 finalization):

* **Sign:** `SIGN_ROBUST` iff no Class A level shows `SIGN_FLIP`, `TO_TIE` or
  `FROM_TIE` against BASE. Otherwise `SIGN_SENSITIVE`.
* **Magnitude bands, on |level − base| / |base|:**

| band | relative movement |
|---|---|
| Highly stable | ≤ 10% |
| Stable | ≤ 25% |
| Moderately sensitive | ≤ 50% |
| Highly sensitive | > 50% |

* `MAGNITUDE_RATIO_UNINFORMATIVE` applies when |base| is within the tie band.
* Class B and additional levels are reported but never label a finding.

### 4.1 Why F1's Stage 1 BASE differs from its certified value

Experiment 1 was certified with crowding on and a widened 243,257-path set. The
Stage 1 evaluator runs the frozen Experiment 6 model instance.

| quantity | Stage 1 | Experiment 1 |
|---|---|---|
| N0 current-plan unserved | 10,423.68 | 10,261.92 |
| seed plans | −5.94 / −6.16 / −5.98% | certified −6.60 / −6.72 / −6.63% |

The sign agrees. Per §14.1, Stage 1 BASE is the classification reference, and
both values are reported.

### 4.2 Supporting detail

* **F2:** the null test breaks at no applicable level. It is not applicable at
  A7_RM09, which removes the route the splice uses.
* **F3, AF1 and A7:** N0 and N3 rank their busiest routes differently at ranks 4
  and 5 (005 against 010, and 010 is the add_stop route). So A7_RM04 and RM05
  compare two different disruptions. Excluding them, F3's label is SIGN_ROBUST.
  The table carries the mismatch flag and the recomputed label.
* **N3 R1_H20 emptiness:**
  * Re-proved INFEASIBLE_UNDER_ENVELOPE at BASE and at the three A3 levels.
  * At every A7 level the minimum-service plan is admitted, giving
    `INFEASIBILITY_NOT_PROVEN`. Removing a route frees the early-period
    peak proxy, so the infeasible-to-feasible transition is a level effect.
* **F5 monotonicity:** Exp 5's preregistered monotone ordering
  (J090 ≥ J100 ≥ J110 and P090 ≥ J100 ≥ P110) changes only at A5_LAM1, on both
  networks.

### 5.2 The objective's boundary (skeptic pass, AGENTS.md rule 9)

The A5_LAM1 F1 value (+182%) and F4 flip were checked against the record
components directly:

| N0 F6 REF | BASE | A5_LAM1 |
|---|---|---|
| objective | 2,940,186 | 1,831,272 |
| generalized cost | 1,757,243 | 69,305 |
| unserved | 9,858 | 29,366 |
| served | 21,091 | 1,583 |
| revenue vehicle-hours | 2,516 | 557 |
| route-periods OFF | 17 | 139 |

* The objective is GC + λ · 60 · unserved, with no operating-cost term.
* At λ = 1 an unserved trip costs 60 while a served trip averages about 83–86
  GC. Re-optimization therefore removes service.
* N3 converges to the same near-empty service, so AF1 = 0 there. N4 (1,132
  vehicle-hours) does likewise.
* The F4 flip at λ = 1, in both stages, is this collapse. It is not a reversal
  of the network comparison inside the well-posed regime.
* Experiment 1's own frontier already showed the same boundary: unserved
  −1.42% at λ = 1 and +6.85% at λ = 0.5, both uncertified (D15).

The milder reversals follow the same mechanism. At these levels the plans keep
using the full 2,515–2,517 vehicle-hours, but serve fewer trips at lower GC per
trip:

| level | REF served | GC per trip |
|---|---|---|
| A5_TP200 | 14,263 | 71.9 |
| A6_MAXWALK75 | 13,537 | 73.1 |
| A6_WALKSPD85 | 18,324 | 80.9 |

Experiment 6 already showed that the objective is flat across plans that
differ by thousands of served trips. These levels tip that flat ridge toward
fewer, cheaper trips.

## 8. Permitted claims

> Under the frozen Model B proxy-demand model and the EXP4N common modeled
> envelope, the certified Experiment 1 frequency plans reduce modeled unserved
> demand relative to the current plan at every implemented Stage 1 assumption
> level (−1.9% to −7.0%).

> Re-optimized under the selected levels, that reduction holds where the
> unserved-trip penalty dominates (λ = 4, transfer penalty ×0.5) and reverses
> where it does not (λ = 1, transfer penalty ×2, both walking levels). The
> objective then trades served trips for lower generalized cost.

> The greenfield network N4 is worse than N3 and N0 at every λ ≥ 2 level in both
> stages. The only reversal is at λ = 1, where re-optimization collapses service
> on every network.

> Policy prices (study safeguards) are non-negative at every level. Their
> rankings are unchanged at transfer penalty ×0.5 and move at λ = 1, transfer
> penalty ×2 and the walking levels.

## 9. Prohibited claims

* Any reliability robustness (A4 UNIMPLEMENTED).
* Robustness to non-commute demand beyond commute OD pairs (A1 scope).
* Robustness of anything to bootstrap re-optimization (A2 not selected).
* Robustness under the jobs-accessibility objective (Class B untested).
* Reading λ = 1 results as evidence against F1 or F4 inside the certified
  λ ≥ 2 regime.
* Served-trip or GC figures as findings in their own right (basin-dependent;
  Exp 6 §7).
* Buses, fleet or deployability; the peak axis is a proxy.
* Per-route headway recommendations.
* Global optimality for any closed cell.
